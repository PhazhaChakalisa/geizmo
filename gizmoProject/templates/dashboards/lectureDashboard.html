📈 Grading Accuracy Metrics

Mean Absolute Error
Average difference between the AI's predicted mark and the actual mark. Lower is better.

• <1.0 = Excellent — within 1 mark
• 1.0–2.0 = Good
• >2.0 = Needs more calibration data
—
MAE (marks) ℹ
Mean Absolute Error
Pearson Correlation
How well the AI's predicted ranks match the actual ranks. Range: -1 to 1. Higher is better.

• 0.9–1.0 = Excellent agreement
• 0.7–0.9 = Good
• <0.7 = Weak — needs more data
—
Pearson r ℹ
Correlation (1.0 = perfect)
Grade Band Accuracy
% of submissions where the AI correctly predicted Pass vs Fail (50% threshold). Higher is better.

• >85% = AI reliably classifies pass/fail
• 70–85% = Acceptable
• <70% = More graded data needed
—
Grade Band Acc. ℹ
Pass/Fail match rate
Key-Point F1 Score
How accurately the AI detects which marking points a student covered. Balances precision and recall. Higher is better.

• >0.80 = Very accurate detection
• 0.60–0.80 = Good
• <0.60 = Add more key-points to model answers
—
Key-Point F1 ℹ
Point detection accuracy
